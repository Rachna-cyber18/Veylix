Why we built Veylix
Fake Links Are Everywhere
Every day, people get fake messages on WhatsApp, Gmail, SMS and social media. These messages have dangerous links that look real — but they steal your passwords, bank details, and personal information.

Most people click on these links within 60 seconds — before they even realize it's a trap. And there's no simple tool that warns them before they click.

What's the Problem?
1. People can't tell real links from fake ones
A fake link like "amaz0n-secure-login.com" looks almost like Amazon. Scammers hide dangerous links inside normal-looking messages, and most people can't spot the difference.

2. Current tools are too complicated
Tools like VirusTotal exist — but they show technical reports that normal people don't understand. You have to copy-paste the link, wait for results, and then figure out what the numbers mean.

3. Nobody warns you BEFORE you click
By the time a link gets reported as dangerous, thousands of people have already clicked on it. There's no tool that catches the link and warns you first.

4. People don't learn from their mistakes
Even if you avoid one scam, you won't know what to look for next time. No tool teaches you WHY a link was dangerous — they just block it and move on.

What is Veylix?
Veylix is an intelligent URL threat detection and risk analysis tool. It scans links received via WhatsApp, Gmail, SMS and social media to detect phishing, malware, and scam attempts before you click. The name "Veylix" combines "Vey" (Verify) and "Lix" (Links) — meaning "Verify your Links."
⚙️
How Does It Work?
Veylix uses a multi-layered analysis approach: pattern matching to detect known phishing patterns, heuristic analysis for suspicious behaviors, domain reputation checking against known threats, and SSL verification to ensure secure connections. Each layer contributes to a risk score from 0–100.
